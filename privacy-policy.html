Privacy Policy

Dawa Info · Last updated: 10 August 2026

Short version: Dawa Info works mainly on your device. We do not require an account, and we do not sell your personal data. Medicine searches and shelf/reminder data stay on your phone unless you choose to share them.

1. Who we are

Dawa Info is a medicine information app published by JMR Apps (developer: Muthu Raj / JMR Apps).

Contact for privacy questions: contact@jmrlifting.com (or the email listed on the Google Play developer page).

2. What the app does

Dawa Info provides general information about medicines (names, composition, common uses, side-effect notes, comparisons, and reminders).

Not medical advice. The app is for informational purposes only. Always consult a qualified doctor or pharmacist before taking or changing any medicine.

3. Data we collect

We do not require you to create an account.

Data stored on your device only (using browser/app local storage) may include:

  • Language and theme preference
  • Favourites / My Shelf list and custom medicine notes you add
  • Reminder settings (medicine name, times, person label)
  • Recent search history on the device

This information stays on your device. Clearing site/app data or uninstalling removes it.

We do not intentionally collect: name, phone number, email, precise location, contacts, photos, or payment details through Dawa Info.

4. How data is used

  • To show your shelf, reminders, and preferences
  • To run search and offline features on your device

We do not use your shelf or reminder content for advertising profiles.

5. Sharing and third parties

  • Share button: If you tap Share, content is sent only through the share method you choose (e.g. WhatsApp). That is under your control and that app’s policy.
  • Hosting: The web app may be hosted on services such as GitHub Pages. Standard server logs (e.g. IP, browser type) may be processed by the host under their policies. The app is designed so medicine shelf data is not uploaded as part of normal use.
  • Notifications: Reminder alerts use your device notification permission if you allow it. We do not send marketing push from our servers in the current version.

We do not sell personal data.

6. Children

Dawa Info is a general information tool. It is not directed at children under 13. Do not submit personal information of children. Health decisions for minors must involve a parent/guardian and a doctor.

7. Security

On-device storage is protected by your device security (lock screen, OS permissions). No method of electronic storage is 100% secure. Avoid entering highly sensitive personal health records into free-text notes if you share the device with others.

8. Your choices

  • Change or clear language, theme, shelf, and reminders inside the app
  • Clear browser/site data or uninstall the app to remove local data
  • Deny notification permission if you do not want reminder alerts

9. Google Play / Android (Trusted Web Activity)

If you install Dawa Info from Google Play as an Android package (e.g. TWA), the same privacy practices apply: core medicine tools run as a web app with local storage. Google Play’s own data practices are described in Google’s policies.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will change. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
